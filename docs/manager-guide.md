# Performance discussions — manager guide

How to run a performance discussion at Capsule and how to record it in the
Performance Dashboard.

This mirrors the **Overview & Guidelines** page in Rippling, and `index.html` in
this folder carries the same content as a working page. That page is the
one managers see; this copy exists so the content can be reviewed and edited
outside the function editor. If you change one, change the other.

Questions about a specific situation, or anything this guide doesn't cover —
peopleops@capsule.com.

---

## Before you start

Discussions about attendance, performance, and behavior are how team members
learn where they stand. Done well, they open a path forward. Done carelessly,
they close one.

Four things worth holding in mind, whatever the discussion is about.

**Assume they can improve.** A performance discussion is not meant to leave
someone feeling scared, inadequate, or devalued. Go in assuming improvement is
possible — the goal is to help them get there.

**Let them speak first.** Give the team member room to reflect on their own
performance before you deliver yours. How they describe the situation tells you
a great deal about their self-awareness and what they understand the job to
require.

**Bring specifics, not impressions.** Dates, times, what was observed, who else
was involved or affected. Specifics keep the conversation on deliverables and
results. Generalizations invite argument about whether the thing even happened.

**Say what support looks like.** Name the coaching, tools, or resources you'll
provide. A discussion that only describes a gap is a verdict; one that
describes a path is an opportunity.

## Where things live

The Performance Dashboard has two tabs you'll use.

### Data Dashboard

Current attendance point balances and discussion history for your team, in two
tables. The filters across the top narrow both at once — by employee, manager,
department, work location, discussion type, warning level, status, or date
range.

Use this to see the shape of things before you act: whether a pattern exists,
whether someone already has an active warning, how recent the last discussion
was.

### Performance Discussion Actions

Where you create and manage discussions. The left sidebar has two views:

- **By Team Member** — where you create and edit. Opens by default.
- **By Department** — a read-only list across a whole department.

### Getting started

1. **Select the team member.** Use the search field at the top of By Team
   Member to find the person this discussion concerns. You won't appear in your
   own results.
2. **Confirm their information.** Title, department, manager, work location,
   start date, and discussion counts appear in the right-hand panel. Give it a
   quick check — if anything's wrong, fix it on their profile first. The panel
   takes a moment to populate; wait for it before choosing an action.
3. **Choose an action.** Create a new discussion, or review existing ones.
   Opening one section hides the other. Each has a switch in case you need to
   hide the section from view.

## Plan a discussion

What you pick at the start determines which sections you'll fill in and how
long the discussion stays in effect. The Rippling page has an interactive
planner; the same information is below.

### Types and reasons

| Type | Available reasons |
| --- | --- |
| Memo of Conversation | Attendance · Performance · Behavior · Misconduct / Violation of Company Policy · Positive Feedback |
| Warning | Attendance · Performance · Behavior · Misconduct / Violation of Company Policy |
| Termination Notice | Attendance · Performance · Behavior · Misconduct / Violation of Company Policy · Job Abandonment |

Type, reason, and level are locked once the record exists. Before you click
**Begin New Discussion**, use **Reset Type, Reason, and/or Level** to change
them. Afterwards, cancel the discussion and start a new one.

### Warning levels

| Level | Becomes | Monitoring period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | Verbal | 90 days |
| Second | Written | 90 days |
| Final | Final Written | 180 days |

Attendance warnings follow the team member's attendance policy instead — 90
days on the Logistics policy, 180 on Standard. The panel shows which one
applies.

### Which sections appear

| Section | Appears for | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feedback Details | Positive Feedback only | Date of feedback, and what was said |
| Incident Details | Everything except Positive Feedback | Date of incident, and what happened |
| Prior Matters | Everything except Positive Feedback | Optional — link any active discussions |
| Additional Information | Termination Notices only | Optional context before the standard summary |
| Action Plan | Everything except Positive Feedback and Termination Notices | Optional — what improvement looks like |
| Proof of Communication | Always | Date, method, and any recording or email |

### Job Abandonment

Instead of a single date of incident, you'll enter three no-call-no-show dates
in order, each on or before the discussion date. The third becomes the date of
incident.

### When the document sends

Automatically on approval — except Termination Notices, which wait until you
confirm you've spoken with the team member. See
[Termination notices](#termination-notices).

## Create a discussion

The form is split into sections. Each one saves on its own.

1. **Fill in the required fields for each section.** Only the sections relevant
   to your type and reason will appear.
2. **Click Validate & Save on each section.** When it succeeds, the button is
   replaced by **Edit** and the fields lock. That's your confirmation the
   section is saved.
3. **Complete the attestation and submit.** Every section must be saved before
   you can submit. **Save** records the attestation without starting approval;
   **Submit** sends it to the People team.

After a successful submit, a green confirmation appears and the Create New
Discussion section clears itself, ready for the next one. The discussion you
just submitted is in Review Existing Discussions.

### Length limits

| Field | Minimum | Paragraphs | Per paragraph |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Feedback Details | 100 characters | up to 9 | under 1,000 characters |
| Incident Details | 100 characters | up to 9 | under 2,000 characters |
| Additional Context | 100 characters | up to 6 | under 1,000 characters |
| Action Plan | 100 characters | up to 6 | under 1,000 characters |

### Date rules

Dates of feedback and incident must fall on or before the discussion's creation
date — and on or before the communication date, if one is set. The three
no-call-no-show dates must be in ascending order with at least a day between
each.

### Proof of communication

A recording is required when the method is **Phone Call** or **Voice Mail** —
those are the only two with no written trail.

### Two things that will trip you up

**Pasting long text can fail validation.** If a section won't save after a
paste, retype a few words anywhere in the text and try again. That's usually
enough.

**Editing a saved section unlocks it again.** Click **Edit**, make your change,
and save that section a second time. Nothing is lost — it just needs
re-confirming.

### Working checklist

For the conversation itself, not the form.

- [ ] I've gathered dates, times, and specifics rather than impressions
- [ ] I've checked their attendance balance and discussion history on the Data Dashboard
- [ ] I've confirmed their details in the right-hand panel are correct
- [ ] I've decided what support or coaching I'm offering
- [ ] I've given the team member room to describe the situation first
- [ ] I've had the conversation before creating the document
- [ ] Every section shows Edit rather than Validate & Save
- [ ] I've read the attestation before checking it

## Review existing discussions

Grouped into four tabs by where each one has got to.

| Status | What it means | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In Progress | Created but not finalized | Keep editing, or approve where you have permission |
| Active | Finalized, with a warning or memo still in effect | Create and send the discussion document |
| Archived | Finalized, monitoring period passed | View only |
| Canceled | Withdrawn before completion | View, and edit if it needs reopening |

The list reflects what was there when the page loaded. If you've saved
something in another tab, use **Click to reload recent changes** above the
tabs.

**View** opens the record itself in a new tab. **Edit/Review** loads it into the
form below, where it behaves the same as a new discussion.

For everything except termination notices, the document sends automatically
once the discussion is approved.

## By department

A read-only view of every discussion in a department, rather than one team
member at a time. Useful for spotting patterns or checking what's outstanding
before a cycle closes.

1. **Click Load departments.** The dropdown stays disabled until you do. The
   list takes a few seconds to build and only needs loading once per visit.
2. **Pick a department and a timeframe.** One department at a time. The
   timeframe limits how far back the search goes — a shorter window returns
   faster.
3. **Narrow by status, if you want to.** Leave Status empty to include
   everything. Draft covers records where no status has been set yet.
4. **Click Load discussions.** Results are listed newest first, each with the
   team member's name. View opens the record in a new tab.

This view is read-only — there's no Edit here. To change a discussion, find it
under By Team Member.

If the message says more records exist, narrow the timeframe. A department with
a long history can hold more than one load can return.

The department list is company-wide, not limited to your own team. Treat what
you see accordingly.

## Termination notices

The one type where the document waits for you.

Every other discussion type sends its document automatically on approval.
Termination notices don't — because the team member needs to hear this from
you, not from an automated email.

1. **You prepare and submit.** Same as any other type.
2. **The People team reviews and approves.** You'll be notified when that
   happens.
3. **You have the conversation.** In person, directly. This step is yours and
   the system can't do it for you.
4. **Then you create the document.** Under Review Existing Discussions, on the
   Active tab: **Send Document**, then **Confirm**.

> Approval means the People team has signed off. It does not mean the team
> member has been told. Creating the document before that conversation notifies
> them before you've had the chance to.

## When something sticks

**The team member panel is empty or still loading.** Give it a few seconds — it
pulls from several places. If it's still blank after that, click **Start Over**
and select the person again. The action buttons stay disabled until the panel
has filled in; that's deliberate, so you don't act on information that isn't
there yet.

**Validation is taking a long time.** If a save runs past about fifteen
seconds, try it again. A long session can slow the page down; reloading the tab
usually clears it. If it keeps happening, send the details to
peopleops@capsule.com.

**A section won't validate and I can't see why.** The message under the section
names the specific problem. The usual causes are text under 100 characters,
more paragraphs than the section allows, or a single paragraph over the
character limit. If you pasted the text in, retype a few words and try again.

**I picked the wrong type or reason.** Before you click **Begin New
Discussion**, use **Reset Type, Reason, and/or Level**. Afterwards those
choices are written to the record and locked — cancel the discussion and start
a new one instead.

**I need to record a discussion about myself.** You can't. You won't appear in
the team member search, and selecting yourself another way is blocked. If a
discussion genuinely needs recording about you, contact
peopleops@capsule.com.

**I want to edit one discussion while drafting another.** You can. Each section
has its own switch, so turning both on puts Create New Discussion and Review
Existing Discussions in view at once. They keep separate state — work in one
won't disturb the other.
